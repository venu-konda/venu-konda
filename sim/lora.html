IoT Sensor Node with LoRa

ESP32 · SX1276 · 865–867 MHz (IN865)

A battery-powered ESP32 node that wakes from deep sleep, reads a sensor, frames a packet with a CRC-16 and transmits it over LoRa to a gateway. The simulator computes LoRa time-on-air from the spreading factor and bandwidth, a log-distance path-loss model gives RSSI and SNR at the gateway, and the receiver sensitivity decides whether each packet gets through. Energy per cycle gives the battery-life estimate.

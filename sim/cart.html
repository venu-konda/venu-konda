Shopping Cart & Bill Payment

C++ · classes · static/const · friend · references GitHub ↗

A browser version of my console C++ application: pick products into a cart, total the bill and pay it from a built-in bank account that checks a password. The account balance and the password-attempt counter are static members, so they are shared by every object and the lock-out survives new menu objects, exactly as in the C++ code.

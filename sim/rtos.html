FreeRTOS Scheduler Visualiser

tasks · queues · semaphores · ISR

A tick-level model of the task set from my RTOS-based control system: a sensor task feeding a queue, a PID control task blocked on that queue, a comms task woken by a UART interrupt through a semaphore, and a low-priority SD-card logger. Change priorities and execution times and see preemption, deadline misses and CPU load on the timeline, the way they would show up on a logic analyser trace of the real firmware.

Priority 4 is highest (FreeRTOS convention). Period applies to periodic tasks; event-driven tasks are released by the queue or the ISR. Exec is the worst-case CPU time per job in ms.

Industrial Data Logger · SD card / FAT32

STM32 · SPI · FatFs · RTC · PT100 · 4–20 mA

A logger that samples an RTD temperature input and a 4–20 mA pressure transmitter on a fixed interval, time-stamps each record from the RTC and appends CSV lines to a daily file on an SD card through FatFs. Records are collected in a 512-byte sector buffer and written with f_write + f_sync, so pulling the card shows what gets lost and what survives.

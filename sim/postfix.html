Infix → Postfix Converter & Evaluator

C · stack · shunting-yard

The stack-based expression parser I wrote in C, step by step: the infix expression is scanned token by token, operators wait on a stack according to precedence and associativity (shunting-yard), operands go straight to the output, and the resulting postfix string is evaluated with a second stack.

Expression

Examples

Supported: integers and decimals, + − * / % ^, parentheses and unary minus. ^ is right-associative, the rest are left-associative. Precedence: ^ > * / % > + −.

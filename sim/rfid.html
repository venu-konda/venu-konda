RFID Attendance System · NXP LPC2129 (ARM7)

UART ISR · I²C EEPROM · RTC · HD44780 LCD GitHub ↗

Present a tag to the reader and follow the firmware: the UART0 receive interrupt collects the tag ID, it is matched against the known list, the person's IN/OUT state is toggled and written to the external I²C EEPROM (so it survives a reset), the event is shown on the 20×4 LCD with the on-chip RTC time, and a CSV record goes out over UART to the Linux host logger.

The menu (external interrupt on P0.16) asks for hour, minute, second, date, month and year. Type two digits, press # to accept, * to cancel.

I²C EEPROM (24C04) · first 16 bytes

One byte per person at 0x00–0x03: 00 = OUT, 01 = IN. Written with a page write after every toggle and read back at boot.

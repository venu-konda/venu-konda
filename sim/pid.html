PID Temperature & Pressure Control

STM32 · FreeRTOS · PT100 · 4–20 mA

A closed-loop PID controller driving a simulated industrial process, as in the temperature and pressure control firmware I wrote on STM32/ESP32. The plant is a first-order system with dead time; the sensor is a PT100 RTD (resistance shown in ohms) or a 4–20 mA pressure transmitter. Tune the gains and watch the response.

How it works

Every Ts the control task samples the sensor, computes e = SP − PV and the output u = Kp·e + Ki·∫e·dt + Kd·d(−PV)/dt, clamped to 0–100 % and written to a timer PWM channel. The derivative acts on the measurement (not the error) through a first-order filter, so setpoint steps do not kick the output, and the integrator is frozen while the output is saturated (anti-windup). In the firmware this runs in a FreeRTOS task woken by a periodic timer, with the ADC/RTD reading taken through an SPI driver and the PWM written to a hardware timer.
